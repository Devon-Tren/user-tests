# NoteNest

Your ideas, organized. NoteNest is a lightweight note-taking app for capturing
drafts quickly and getting them out again.

## Features

- **Create an account** — sign up with your email and start taking notes in seconds.
- **Draft notes** — jot ideas down and save them as drafts to finish later.
- **Export** — export all of your notes to a portable JSON file with one click,
  so your data is never locked in. *(This is the planted goal gap: the feature
  is prominently documented here but does not exist in the app.)*
- **Sharing** — share notes with teammates (coming soon).

## Goals

1. Onboarding should take under a minute.
2. No user should ever lose a draft.
3. Data portability: users can always get their notes out (see **Export** above).
